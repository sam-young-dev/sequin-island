Your tax-deductible gift plays a vital role in preserving the Seguin Island Light Station and ensuring this historic beacon remains accessible for generations to come. Every contribution strengthens the ongoing mission of the Friends of Seguin Island Light Station.

By donating, you become part of the family here at Friends of Seguin Island Light Station.

### Gifts of Stock

### Donated Items

To coordinate donated goods or materials, contact Lauren O'Reilly, Executive Assistant to the Board, at [keeper@seguinisland.org](mailto:keeper@seguinisland.org) or 207-442-4808.

### Employer Matching Gifts

Many employers offer matching gift programs that can double or even triple the impact of your contribution. Consult your employer's program administrator to initiate a match.

Friends of Seguin Island Light Station endorses this [Donor Bill of Rights](https://afpglobal.org/sites/default/files/attachments/2018-10/DonorBillofRights.pdf).

_Friends of Seguin Island Light Station is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Donations and membership contributions are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law._
