Ahoy, friends! This past weekend was full with summer hosting on Seguin with perfect weather and perfect company. Our cove was a sight to see with full moorings and the crew of the [Harvey Gamage](https://www.sailboston.com/ships/harvey-gamage/). We are honored to be your preferred island choice for a perfect picnic stop!

![Harvey Gamage boat in the cove](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0933-1024x768.jpeg)

This week is busy beyond hosting summer memories; we are working on tower improvements! Any project on an island is no small feat and maintaining a lighthouse tower is no exception. We are so fortunate to work with an incredible crew who are no strangers to island repairs and lighthouse projects.

![Four members of the tower crew sitting on the steps of a white clapboard porch beside a brick wall](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_2420-768x1024.jpeg)

**This does unfortunately mean that tower tours are unavailable through Friday, July 31st!** Fortunately our beautiful views are not canceled and we have several Seguin adventures we recommend outside of going up the tower.

## Explore Picnic at the point of the original tower

One of the best whale watching points on the island is where the original tower was built! Pack a picnic and take in the ocean view. Peak behind you to see the First Order Fresnel lens getting a paint improvement!

![Open ocean to the horizon, seen over bayberry and tall summer grass from the point](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0964-1024x768.jpeg)

## Explore the artifacts around the fog signal building

We have a flywheel and other found artifacts around the side of the fog signal building. Continue around and see where the current fog sensor and signal are. There’s a small bench and beautiful overlook of the South Trail here as well.

![The brick fog signal building, with two large rusted iron wheels resting in the grass beside it](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0963-1024x768.jpeg)

## See the first order Fresnel lens from a new perspective

One of our favorite vantage points of the first order Fresnel lens is by the signpost on your way to the fog signal building. Enjoy whale watching from this bench and take in the 9’3″ dramatic lens that Seguin is proud to share.

![The lighthouse lantern and brick keeper's house seen across tall grass, beside a wooden signpost of mileage signs](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0962-1-1024x768.jpeg)

## Take a berry walk on the North Trail

Blueberries and raspberries are in season and the best spot is the North Trail. This trail loops and has a beautiful vantage point of the cove below.

![A two-masted schooner anchored in the cove, seen over green shrubs from the North Trail](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0931-1-768x1024.jpeg)

We are missing our tower walks too but look forward to returning after this essential project. Thank you for your patience and see you soon!
