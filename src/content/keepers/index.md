Want to know what it takes to be a keeper at Seguin Island Light Station? Start as a Wednesday Warrior. It may just improve your chances.

Friends of Seguin Island Light Station looks for volunteers to relieve the keepers and take on maintenance one day a week, with a free boat ride out to the island. The work may include:

* Scraping and painting buildings and windows
* Weed whacking
* Driving the zero-turn lawn mower
* Helping with retail sales, museum tours, and lighthouse tours

### What you'll need

* Sign up in advance, so the boat captain knows you're coming.
* Be fit enough to hike the 180-foot path up to the lighthouse and do any of the work above.
* Be at the dock at Fort Popham by 7:45 am for the prompt 8:00 am departure, no exceptions. The boat ride is 2.5 miles.
* Bring your own drinking water, lunch or snacks, bug repellent, sunscreen, work gloves, and a jacket, because the weather always changes. A change of clothes is a good idea in case you get wet.
* Wear work boots or similar shoes.

To sign up or ask a question, call Tom McNamara at [703-801-6899](tel:+17038016899) before any Wednesday.

To keep the island for a season, read the [Keeper-Caretaker Agreement (PDF)](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2026-Lighthouse-Keeper-Caretaker-Agreement-1.pdf) and send in the [Keeper-Caretaker Application (Word document)](https://seguinisland.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FOSILS-Keeper-Caretaker-Application-Hall-edit-v9.22.2026.docx).
