## Overall Impression

The copy is warm and on-brand, and the alternative giving paths (mail, stock, items, employer match) are complete and genuinely useful. But the page that exists specifically to ask for money reads exactly like the pages that don't — same template, same gray button, same flat prose treatment as About or Contact. The single biggest opportunity: the home page has already solved this problem once (the `.support` band) and the Donate page doesn't reuse any of that thinking.

## What's Working

1. **On-voice copy.** "By donating, you become part of the family here at Friends of Seguin Island Light Station" matches the site's warm, first-person-plural voice, and the tax-deductible framing appears in the very first sentence.
2. **Complete alternative-giving coverage.** Mail, stock transfer (with a named contact), donated items (with a named contact), and employer matching cover the realistic range of ways a mid-size nonprofit receives gifts.
3. **Legitimate trust material exists.** The 501(c)(3) statement and Donor Bill of Rights link are present and accurate — they're just misplaced (see below).

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The Donate button doesn't use Keeper's Brick, the design system's own designated color for this exact action.**
- **Why it matters:** DESIGN.md states Brick "is spent on the support action (Donate) and nothing else competes with it on the same screen." The button in `donate.md` line 10 (`<a class="button" href="https://seguinisland.ejoinme.org/donations">Donate Now</a>`) has no `data-button-variant="support"`, so it falls back to Granite Gray — identical to every other button sitewide, including the header's Brick "Donate" link that routes here. The one page whose entire job is the donate action fails to visually distinguish it at all.
- **Fix:** Add `data-button-variant="support"` to the Donate Now button in `donate.md`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P0] Five giving methods presented with equal visual weight; no hierarchy separates the primary online gift from the four alternatives.**
- **Why it matters:** Fails 6 of 8 cognitive-load checklist items (single focus, grouping, visual hierarchy, one-thing-at-a-time, minimal choices, progressive disclosure). A ready-to-give donor faces an undifferentiated wall of options instead of one obvious next step.
- **Fix:** Give the online gift its own visually distinct block (echoing `.support__actions`/`.support__reassurance`, already built on the home page), and visually demote "Additional Ways to Give" — smaller heading scale, tinted band, or a collapsed section.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Trust signals (501(c)(3) status, tax-deductibility, Donor Bill of Rights) sit at the very bottom, disconnected from the ask.**
- **Why it matters:** Peak-end rule — reassurance a first-time donor needs should sit at the point of decision, not after four paragraphs of mail/stock/item/matching logistics. Right now the page's strongest trust asset arrives last, in italic small type, diluted.
- **Fix:** Pull a short reassurance line directly under the Donate Now button, matching the pattern `.support__reassurance` already establishes on the home page.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] No hero, no imagery, no visual distinction from About/Contact despite being the site's most conversion-critical page.**
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md names "Support is the through-line" as a core principle, and this is explicitly a Persuade-mode surface where "design is the product" — yet the page is template-identical to a static content page, a visual downshift exactly when momentum should build (especially for visitors arriving via the header's Brick Donate link or a blog post).
- **Fix:** Give Donate the same design investment already proven on the home page's `.support` band, rather than leaving it in raw `.prose flow`. Worth deciding first whether this warrants a bespoke template like `/visit` and `/map` have.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`

**[P3] Phone numbers aren't tappable; the external ejoinme link gives no "opens in a new tab" cue.**
- **Why it matters:** Minor but real friction for a mobile donor who wants to call about a stock gift, and for anyone unsure whether they've left FOSILS's site once the tab opens.
- **Fix:** Wrap phone numbers in `tel:` links; add a small visible external-link indicator on the Donate Now button.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Arrives via the header's Brick-colored Donate link expecting continuity — and lands on a page where the button is now plain gray, identical to every other button on the site. The color cue that got Jordan here disappears exactly when reassurance matters most. Jordan then has to read past four "By Mail / Gifts of Stock / Donated Items / Employer Matching" paragraphs before reaching the 501(c)(3) legitimacy proof at the very bottom — by which point they may have already bounced.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** Deliberately looks for legitimacy proof before giving. The 501(c)(3) determination and Donor Bill of Rights exist, but they're the last two lines on the page, in italic, after four operational paragraphs about checks and stock transfers — backwards order for this persona's actual decision path.

**Casey (Mobile User):** Taps Donate Now — fine, big touch target, opens ejoinme in a new tab with no visible "leaving this site" warning, so a slow-loading ejoinme page could read as broken. If Casey instead wants to call Tip about a stock gift, "207-522-1690" is plain text, not `tel:` — no tap-to-call, real friction for someone already distracted.

## Minor Observations

- The h3 subheadings ("By Mail," "Gifts of Stock," "Donated Items," "Employer Matching Gifts") mix preposition-led, noun-led, and gerund-led phrasing — a small copy-polish opportunity.
- The Donor Bill of Rights link opens a PDF with no file-type indicator.
- `.prose`'s 70ch/1.65-line-height treatment (the "Letter from the Island Rule") is correct for long-form history content but may be the wrong register applied wholesale to a persuasion page.

## Questions to Consider

- If Keeper's Brick is designated as "the one support action" color, why does the header's shortcut *to* Donate get to use it while the actual Donate button doesn't?
- The home page's `.support` band already does more persuasive work in four lines of markdown than this entire 36-line page — should `/donate` be redesigned to look like the destination that band promises, or should the band simply absorb the ask and `/donate` become a lighter "more ways to give" appendix?
- Given "Support is the through-line" and this page's Persuade-mode status, does routing it through the same generic `[slug].astro` template as About/Contact still make sense, or does its conversion-critical role justify a bespoke template the way `/visit` and `/map` already have?
